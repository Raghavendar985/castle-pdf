How Castle PDF works

Your file stays put

Everything runs inside your browser tab. Your PDFs and images are processed on your device and are never uploaded to a server.

Pick the piece for the job

The Pawn turns images into a PDF, the Knight shrinks a file, the Bishop cuts pages out and the Queen merges files into one.

Check, then save

Each result shows its file size before you save it to your device.

Questions

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens in your browser, so your files never leave your device. The page does load its PDF libraries and fonts from public content networks when it opens, but your documents are not part of that.

Do I need an account?

No. Open a tool, add your file and save the result.

Why can't I select text in a compressed PDF?

Compress redraws every page as an image to reduce its size, so the text becomes part of the picture. Merge and Split do not do this, and they keep text selectable.

Can I use a password-protected PDF?

Not yet. Remove the password in the app that created the file, then try again.

Is there a file size limit?

Castle PDF sets none. The limit is your device memory, so very large files take longer and may fail on a phone.

Which image formats work?

JPG, PNG, WebP and GIF. Phone photos are rotated the right way up, and transparent areas become white.
