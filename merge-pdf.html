    How to merge PDF files

    1. Add two or more PDFs. Click Choose PDFs or drop them on the box.
    2. Set the order with the arrow buttons. The first file in the list comes first in the result.
    3. Click Merge PDFs.
    4. Save merged.pdf.

    Questions

    Does merging change the quality?

    No. Pages are copied as they are, so text stays selectable and images keep their quality.

    Do bookmarks and form fields carry over?

    They may not. Castle PDF copies the pages, so check the result if your files rely on bookmarks or fillable fields.

    How many files can I merge?

    Castle PDF sets no limit. The limit is your device memory, so very large files take longer and may fail on a phone.

    Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

    Not yet. Remove the password in the app that created each file, then try again.

    Are my files uploaded anywhere?

    No. Everything happens in your browser, so your files never leave your device. The page does load its PDF libraries and fonts from public content networks when it opens, but your documents are not part of that.
