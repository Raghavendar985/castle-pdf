Compress a PDF

Each page is re-drawn at a lower resolution and saved as a JPEG. Scanned files and photo-heavy PDFs shrink the most.

    Quality

    Compressed pages become images, so the text in the result cannot be selected or searched. Keep your original if you need that.

    ♚︎The king is your file. It stays on your device and is never uploaded.

    How to compress a PDF

    1. Choose your PDF. Click Choose a PDF or drop the file on the box.
    2. Pick a quality. Smallest uses 72 dpi and JPEG 50, Balanced uses 100 dpi and JPEG 65, and Sharp uses 144 dpi and JPEG 82.
    3. Click Compress PDF and wait while each page is processed.
    4. Compare the original and result sizes, then click Save PDF.

    Questions

    How much smaller will my PDF get?

    It depends on the file. Scans and PDFs full of photos usually shrink a lot. A PDF that is mostly text may not shrink, or may even grow, because each page becomes an image. Castle PDF shows the before and after size so you can decide.

    Can I search the text in a compressed PDF?

    No. Compressed pages are images, so the text cannot be selected or searched. If you need searchable text, keep the original, or use Split and Merge, which keep text selectable.

    Which quality should I choose?

    Start with Balanced. Use Smallest when you have a tight size limit, such as an email attachment. Use Sharp when small print must stay readable.

    Can I compress a password-protected PDF?

    Not yet. Remove the password in the app that created the file, then try again.

    Are my files uploaded anywhere?

    No. Everything happens in your browser, so your files never leave your device. The page does load its PDF libraries and fonts from public content networks when it opens, but your documents are not part of that.
