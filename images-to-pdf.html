Images to PDF

Turn JPG, PNG, WebP and GIF images, such as photos, scans and screenshots, into one PDF, one image per page, in the order you set.

    Image quality

    ♚︎The king is your file. It stays on your device and is never uploaded.

    How to turn images into a PDF

    1. Add your images. Click Choose images or drop them on the box. You can add many at once.
    2. Set the order. Use the arrow buttons to move an image up or down. Each image becomes one page.
    3. Choose the page size and quality. Match the image makes each page the size of its picture. A4 and Letter fit every image on a standard page, with an optional 10 mm margin.
    4. Click Create PDF, then Save PDF.

    Questions

    Which image types can I use?

    JPG, PNG, WebP and GIF. HEIC photos from some iPhones may not open in every browser, so export them as JPG first.

    Will my phone photos come out the right way up?

    Yes. Castle PDF reads the rotation stored in each photo and applies it.

    Does the quality setting change my pictures?

    Yes. Each image is saved as a JPEG at the size and quality you pick. High keeps up to 2400 pixels on the longest side, Medium 1600 and Small 1100. Choose High for photos you plan to print.

    What happens to transparent PNG images?

    Transparent areas become white.

    Are my files uploaded anywhere?

    No. Everything happens in your browser, so your files never leave your device. The page does load its PDF libraries and fonts from public content networks when it opens, but your documents are not part of that.
