Split a PDF

Pull out the pages you need, or cut the file into equal parts. Text stays selectable and searchable.

    Mode

    The last file holds whatever pages remain.

    ♚︎The king is your file. It stays on your device and is never uploaded.

    How to split a PDF

    1. Choose your PDF. The page count shows under the file name.
    2. Choose Extract pages and type the pages you want, like 1-3, 5, 8-. Or choose Split into parts and set how many pages go in each file.
    3. Click Split PDF.
    4. Save the new PDF, or the ZIP file when you split into parts.

    Questions

    How do I write the page numbers?

    Separate pages with commas and use a dash for a range. 1-3, 5 keeps pages 1, 2, 3 and 5. An open range such as 8- runs to the last page.

    Can I save every page as its own file?

    Yes. Choose Split into parts and set pages per file to 1. You get a ZIP with one PDF for each page.

    Why do I get a ZIP file?

    Splitting into parts makes several PDFs, so they are bundled into one ZIP file that you unpack on your device.

    Does splitting change the quality?

    No. Pages are copied as they are, so text stays selectable and images keep their quality.

    Are my files uploaded anywhere?

    No. Everything happens in your browser, so your files never leave your device. The page does load its PDF libraries and fonts from public content networks when it opens, but your documents are not part of that.
